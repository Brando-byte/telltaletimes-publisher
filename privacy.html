Privacy Policy Last updated: October 3, 2026 Summary TellTaleTimes Publisher ("the tool") is a private tool with one user: the owner of the TellTaleTimes channel. It does not collect data from the public, from viewers or from any other account. What the tool accesses When the channel's owner authorizes the tool on a platform, it receives: Access and refresh tokens for the channel's own account. Basic profile information for that account (such as its ID and display name), used to confirm the right account is connected. Permission to upload videos to that account. How it is used This information is used only to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts and to record the links of the resulting posts. Where it is stored Tokens are stored in a file with restricted permissions on a private computer controlled by the channel's owner. They are not stored anywhere else. Sharing Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Data is sent only to the platform it came from, as required to upload a video. Cookies and tracking This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Retention and deletion Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the tool. Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), and the stored tokens are then deleted.
